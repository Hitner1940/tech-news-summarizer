# Gemma 4 E4B at IQ2_XXS: Tensor-Level Precision Redistribution Recovers 96.74% Reasoning at 24% of BF16 Size | Gemma 4 E4B 於 IQ2_XXS：張量級精度重分配置在 BF16 尺寸 24% 下恢復 96.74% 推理能力

## 1. 📌 Executive Reconstruction / 核心重構摘要

**English Briefing:**
An independent investigation by ByteOtter (hosted on r/LocalLLaMA) demonstrates that IQ2_XXS quantization of Gemma 4 E4B — when paired with category-oriented imatrix calibration followed by tensor-level precision redistribution — recovers reasoning performance from 28.9 to 69.5 (+140.54% relative) while consuming approximately 3.3 GB, roughly 24% of the original BF16 footprint. No post-training, LoRA fine-tuning, pruning, or weight updates were applied. The result stands as the first empirical evidence that intelligent weight-bit allocation can partially reverse the catastrophic collapse typically observed at extreme quantization levels. Eleven of eleven evaluated capability categories showed point-estimate improvement over the imatrix-only baseline; only stability-regressed.

## 2. 🔬 Architectural Deep-Dive / 底層架構剖析

### 2.1 Quantization Methodology: KQuants IQ2_XXS
The IQ2_XXS format belongs to the KQuants family (llama.cpp). It operates by splitting tensor groups into sub-blocks allocated across multiple bit-paths: a 2-bit primary path, a 1-bit secondary path, and overhead bits for scale factors. The total budget per token-group averages approximately 0.55–0.65 bits-per-weight (BPW), depending on grouping size. At this extreme compression ratio, uniform quantization collapses nearly all semantic signal; therefore, the preservation mechanism hinges on **non-uniform allocation**.

### 2.2 imatrix Calibration Pipeline
The imatrix (importance matrix) is constructed from a **category-oriented corpus** — a deliberately curated subset targeting specific capability domains (reasoning, math, coding, etc.). Unlike standard corpus-agnostic calibrations, this approach encodes per-category Hessian importance scores, which directly inform which tensors carry task-critical gradient information. The critical insight: not all attention heads or MLP sub-layers are equally destructive when under-quantized.

### 2.3 Tensor-Level Precision Redistribution
Under a fixed byte budget (~3.3 GB for Gemma 4 E4B at IQ2_XXS), the algorithm:
1. Computes per-tensor importance via the imatrix.
2. Allocates higher bit-paths to tensors scoring above a category-specific threshold.
3. Accepts heavier quantization on low-importance tensors (e.g., certain norm layers or low-rank projections).
4. Iteratively re-balances until the total size constraint is satisfied.

This is fundamentally a **knapsack optimization problem** over tensor groups with accuracy constraints — a departure from the flat quantization (uniform BPW across all layers) used in standard GGUF exports.

### 2.4 Why Reasoning Survives at 0.6 BPW
Reasoning-capable layers (deep transformer blocks, particularly those involved in chain-of-thought routing) tend to accumulate higher imatrix scores because their activations drive multi-step logical inference. By preferentially preserving these tensors at slightly higher effective BPW, the model retains its core deductive machinery even as peripheral capacity collapses.

## 5. 📈 Strategic & Ecosystem Implications / 產業生態戰略影響

### 5.1 The End of "One BPW Fits All"
This result invalidates the industry shorthand that "below 2 BPW, reasoning is dead." The KQuants IQ2_XXS path, when combined with category-aware imatrix calibration, proves that **precision redistribution is a free approximation** — it costs nothing in compute or training beyond the calibration phase, yet recovers ~140% of collapsed reasoning capacity.

### 5.2 Implications for On-Device LLM Deployment
A 3.3 GB reasoning-capable model opens doors previously reserved for 7B+FP16 systems:
- **Consumer laptops** (integrated GPU territory) can now run multi-step reasoning workloads.
- **Mobile edge inference** becomes plausible for reasoning-dominant tasks (code review, logical QA, summarization of structured inputs).
- **Cost-per-inference** drops dramatically when serving models no longer require A100-class hardware.

### 5.3 Threat to Closed-Source API Monopoly
If a 4B-parameter model at 24% of BF16 size retains 96.74% of reasoning capability, the economic argument for paying per-token API rates weakens for reasoning-heavy workloads. Enterprises can self-host competitive reasoning pipelines at a fraction of cloud inference costs.

### 5.4 Open Questions & Research Gaps
1. **Generalization:** Does the imatrix-calibration approach transfer across model architectures (Llama, Qwen, Mistral family)?
2. **Dynamic allocation:** Can per-request precision routing (routing high-importance tensors to higher BPW on-the-fly) further recover the lost categories (math, coding, structured output)?
3. **Training-time co-optimization:** Would a quantization-aware pre-training stage amplify the allocation benefit, or is calibration alone near the Pareto frontier?
4. ** reproducibility:** Independent replication of the 140.54% reasoning recovery remains the single most valuable next step for the community.
