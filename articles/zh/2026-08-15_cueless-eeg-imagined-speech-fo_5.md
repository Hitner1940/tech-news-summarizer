- Dataset contains over 4,350 trials from 11 subjects across five sessions
- Evaluated SVM, XGBoost, EEG Conformer, and Shallow ConvNet classifiers
- Achieved outstanding classification accuracy of 97.93%
- Demonstrates strong potential for secure real-world brain-computer interface applications
